A grown-up but playful look for a couple's expense tracker: oxblood, candy, cobalt and butter on cream. Rebuilt around how you actually use the app now that Claude logs a lot of it, while adding expenses by hand stays one tap away.

1 · What the app is for now

Three jobs, in this order

Expenses arrive two ways: you add them, or Claude logs them from chat. Either way, the app's real work is helping you two check and decide.

Log it

By hand with the + button, or through Claude. Both land in the same place, tagged with who logged them.

Check it

Confirm what Claude logged, catch duplicates, see how you're pacing against budget.

Choose the card

Know which card to use next across both of you, and when bills are due.

Tab bar today

Home Wallets Activity Budgets Categories

Categories gets a whole tab but is rarely touched. Card tracking, the app's best feature, has none.

Suggested

Home Activity Cards Wallets

The + stays centre stage for adding by hand. Budgets fold into Home. Categories, Recurring, Household and Claude settings move to the profile menu.

Rule 1

Numbers on oxblood are cream

Never pink. Butter is allowed for one small highlight, like a pace marker or a pill.

Rule 2

One oxblood block per screen

It holds the number that matters most on that screen. Everything else sits on cream and card.

Rule 3

Tints for areas, full colour for small things

Big fills use tints. Candy, cobalt and butter at full strength are for badges, the + button and pills.

Rule 4

No outlines

Cards separate by fill and spacing. Only things that float (the tab bar, card art) get a soft shadow.

Contrast check

Calculated live with the WCAG formula. 4.5 passes for body text; 3 passes for large, bold text only.

Claude's entries arrive tagged and waiting for a one-tap confirm. Anything you add by hand skips it.

Home · Add

Cards · Add

A running "who owes whom" from splits and repayments, with a one-tap repayment.

6 · Mascot

Flapjack, aka Flappy

To be developed in ChatGPT from your references. The prompts below now use the Cherry Cola colours. Attach both reference images when you paste prompt 1.

What makes these work

  • 1The silhouette is the character. One simple, slightly irregular shape you'd recognise in solid black.
  • 2One flat colour, no outlines. No gradients, shading or texture.
  • 3A tiny face, set slightly high. Dot eyes or sideways glances, a small mouth, optional blush.
  • 4Emotion comes from eyes and squash. Glances, sleepy lashes, a tilt, a stretch.
  • 5Cute but a bit weird. Charming, never babyish.
